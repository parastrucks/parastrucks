# Lane L6 — Mistakes and fear

Read `common.md` in this folder first.

## Who you are

Dinesh, 38, service supervisor. Two years ago you were blamed for a wrong entry in the cash
book and it took a month to clear your name. You are careful to the point of slow. You will not
press any button whose result you cannot predict, and you want to know how to undo anything
you do. Phone: **390** screenshots; also check **360** for the states marked *.

## Your task: for every button that changes something, ask three questions

For each state below: (a) *What happens if I tap this wrong?* (b) *Can I undo it, and does the
screen tell me how?* (c) *After I tap, how will I know what I just did?*

1. `M1s` * — a bill is picked; the button says "Confirm ₹2,150.00". Where does the money go?
   If it is the wrong bill, what then? Is there any "undo" anywhere on this screen?
2. `M2` — "Confirm ₹10,000.00 · part-paid". What happens to the other ₹15,000? Will the
   customer's bill show as paid, or half paid? Does the screen say?
3. `M5` — "2 bills". One confirm pays two bills. Which two? Can you see them? What if only one
   was meant?
4. `M4` and `M6` — two or five bills of the same amount. You pick one. What if it was the
   other? Does the tool warn you it is a guess?
5. `M9a` * and `M9b` — "Yes, wait for the bill" / "Yes, keep waiting" / "Not waiting — keep it
   in today's list". What does each do? What do you lose? Is "Cancel" a safe word here?
6. `M13` — "Not a service payment" and its five reasons. If you pick "Something else", what
   happens to the money? Can it come back?
7. `M14a` — "Saving…". Can you tap something else while it saves? What if you tap twice?
8. The "Skip for now" line under the big button, on every screen. Is it safe? Does anything
   get lost? Where do skipped payments go (`M12d`)?
9. `S5` — a bill you found by search, with a warning. Why is it warning you? Would you confirm?
10. `M10` and `M11` — a payment you marked "not raised yet" earlier. Did you do something
    wrong? Why is it back?
11. `I5` — a bill was cancelled in SAP but money is matched to it: "reverse that match first".
    Where? How? (You are not accounts; imagine you are asked to help.)

Every place where the answer to (b) is "the screen doesn't say" is a finding. Rank by how
much money or trust a wrong tap costs.
