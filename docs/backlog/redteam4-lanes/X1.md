What I was trying to do: find where "Pick a bill above" / "Confirm" sits when accounts clears the queue at a PC.

Measured: `.pay-confirmbar` at 1280×800 = x 574, width **132**, y 677 (M6, M4, M7, M8, M17, E2); 148 wide in M1s/M16 ("Confirm ₹1,930.00"), 159 in S4. `.pay-confirm` = 106px wide. Why: the `@media (min-width: 721px)` rule sets `left: 50%; right: auto; transform: translateX(-50%)` and `max-width: 560px` but never a `width`. A fixed element with `right: auto` and no width is shrink-to-fit, so the bar is as wide as its longest label. How bad: Confuses
What I'd change: `width: 100%` (so `max-width: 560px` takes effect) and, at ≥760px, `left: calc(var(--sidebar-w) + (100% - var(--sidebar-w)) / 2)` so the bar is centred on the column the cards are in. Add a self-test assertion that the bar's rendered width ≥ 320 at 1280.
