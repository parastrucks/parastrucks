# Lane L6 — Mistakes and fear (Dinesh, 38, service supervisor)

I was blamed once for a wrong entry in the cash book. It took a month to clear. So on every
screen here I asked three things: what if I tap this wrong, can I undo it, and how will I know
what I just did. Where the screen does not answer, that is my finding. Worst first.

### L6-1: After I confirm, there is no way back and the screen never says so
State: M1s   Screenshot: M1s-390.png, M1s-390-s1.png, M1s-360-s1.png, M14a-390.png   Viewport: 390 (also 360)
Who I am: Service supervisor who will not press a button he cannot reverse.
What I was trying to do: Match the ₹2,150 from JAYESH PATEL to the only bill shown and press "Confirm ₹2,150.00".
What I expected: Either a line under the button saying "You can undo this from ..." or, after saving, a line like "Matched ₹2,150 to DEMO JAY BATTERIES — Undo".
What I actually saw (quote the screen): "Confirm ₹2,150.00" / "Skip for now". Scrolled to the end: nothing else. Nowhere on the screen is the word undo, reverse, or "last matched". During "Saving…" (M14a) the screen still says "9 left to match" and then I assume the next payment appears, so the one I just did is gone from view with no record I can see.
How bad: Blocks
What I'd change (words or a plain description, no code): After every confirm, show one line at the top of the next payment for a while: "Done: ₹2,150.00 → DEMO JAY BATTERIES (MPI…0412). Undo". And a small "Matched today (3)" link somewhere so I can look back at what I did this morning before I go home. Until that exists I would press "Skip for now" on anything I was not 100% sure of, and the pile would never shrink.
What that pushes down the page: One line; the payment card moves one line lower.
