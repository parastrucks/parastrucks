# Lane L5 — Simple English

Who I am, for every finding below: a plain-language editor writing for Gujarati/Hindi-first
readers who read English at about 8th-grade level. I worked from all 46 text dumps and the
390 screenshots of M6, M7, M8, I3 and I5.

Ranked by how often the words are seen (Match screen first, Import tab last), then by harm.

---

## A. Words seen on every Match screen (20+ times a day)

### L5-1: "Bill not raised yet" — "raised" is a software/accounts word; the mechanic says "made"
State: M1   Screenshot: M6-390-s1.png (button row)   Viewport: 390
Who I am: plain-language editor
What I was trying to do: tell a service exec what the third button does.
What I expected: a button that says, in his words, "the bill is not made yet".
What I actually saw (quote the screen): `Bill not raised yet` (M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6, M7, M8,
M13, M14a, M15, M16, M17, M18, S5). On a waiting payment it becomes `Still not raised` (M9b, M10,
M11). The pop-up then says "Wait for its bill?" — a third way to say the same thing.
How bad: Confuses
What I'd change: `Bill not made yet` on the button; `Bill still not made` on waiting payments.
Keep "made" everywhere the idea appears ("No bill made for this yet"). Never use "raised".
What that pushes down the page: nothing — same length.

### L5-2: The page question "Which bill did each UPI payment pay?" reads backwards

### L5-3: The reason lines under each bill use "exact", "payer", "similar" and the "·" dot
State: M6   Screenshot: M6-390.png   Viewport: 390
Who I am: plain-language editor
What I was trying to do: understand, in one glance, why the tool is showing me this bill.
What I expected: one plain reason per line, in words a mechanic uses.
What I actually saw (quote the screen):
- `Only bill at this exact amount` (M1, M5, M15, M17, M11)
- `Payer name matches the customer` (M2, M11, M17)
- `Same amount as 4 other bills · Payer name is similar` (M6, M16)
- `Only bill at this exact amount · Payer name matches the customer` (M17 — 11 words with a dot in the middle)
- `Found by search · billed 12 Oct` (S5)
"Exact" adds nothing (it is either the same amount or not). "Payer" is a bank word; the reader
says "the name". The "·" dot is silent — a slow reader runs the two reasons into one sentence.

- `Name matches the bill`
- `Name is close to the bill`
- Two reasons → two short lines, or join with "and": `Same amount as 4 other bills. Name is close.`
- `Found by search. Billed 12 Oct`
What that pushes down the page: a second reason line adds one line per bill; on M6/M16 only the
first bill has two reasons, so ~20 px.

## What works

  the M7 line "These bills are equally likely — the list is not ranked. Pick by name, or skip." is
  long but it stops a wrong tap, so it earns its words.

  check against the file, and "Nothing is saved until you have seen what it will change" is the
  one line that makes accounts feel safe.

## Appendix — Wordlist (use these words on every screen)

| Term | Use it for | Do not use |
|---|---|---|
| **payment** | money that came by QR/UPI (one row in the bank file) | receipt, transaction, credit, UPI payment (except once in the subtitle) |
| **bill** | what the workshop charged the customer (SAP invoice) | invoice, document, feed |
| **bill number** | the 10-digit number, e.g. 0099000412 | the long MPI/PCR/LCS code (call that "SAP code" if shown) |
| **job** | one job card that has 2 bills (parts + labour) | — |
| **match** (verb) / **matched** | tie a payment to a bill; a payment that has been tied | allocate, settle, link |
| **confirm** | the button that saves your match | OK, submit, apply |
| **part of** | a payment smaller than its bill: "₹10,000 as part of ₹25,000" | part-paid, partial, left of |
| **still to match** | money from a payment not yet tied to a bill | left, remaining, balance |
| **unpaid bill** | a bill the customer has not fully paid | open bill, outstanding |
| **skip** / **skipped** | leave this payment for later today; it stays in the list | defer, park, waiting |
| **bill not made yet** | the customer paid before the bill was made in SAP | not raised, pending invoice |
| **waiting** / **waiting for its bill** | a payment that left today's list until a new bill of its amount is loaded | queued, on hold (use "on hold" only for parts returns) |
| **comes back** | a waiting payment returning to the list | returns by itself, re-appears |
| **load** / **loaded** | putting a bill or payment file into the tool (the tab may still be called Import) | import, upload, ingest, feed, land, store |
| **today's bills** | the latest Billing Report loaded | fresh bills, invoice feed |
| **out of date** | today's bills are not loaded yet, so confirm is off | stale, disabled |
| **name** | the name on the payment or the bill | payer, customer (use "customer" only for the person) |
| **name matches / name is close** | the two names are the same / nearly the same | similar, fuzzy |
| **not a service payment** | money that is not for a workshop bill (accounts decides) | unrelated, non-service |
| **failed payment** | a QR scan that did not send money | failed attempt, not money |
| **does not agree** | a bill in the file that disagrees with the saved one | clash, conflict |
| **changed** | a saved bill whose amount changed in the new file | amended, updated |
| **QR** / **UPI** / **SAP** / **HDFC** / **.xlsx** | keep: the reader knows QR and UPI; SAP, HDFC and .xlsx name real files — use them only on the Import tab | — |
